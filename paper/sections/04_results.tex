\section{Reduced-Corpus Engineering Pilot}\label{sec:pilot}
% Claims: PILOT-BM25-001, PILOT-BM25-002
The archived BM25 pilot contains \ResultPilotQueries{} queries and
\ResultPilotProducts{} products. It includes every target product plus
hash-sampled distractors, so its difficulty differs from the complete corpus.
The core-specification representation obtains Recall@10 of
\ResultCoreSpecsRecall{}. Adding the final mapped-field group changes
Recall@10 by \ResultFullMinusCoreRecall{}.

These observations apply only to the fixed reduced corpus, field-group order,
and value-only renderer. They are not full-benchmark results, do not isolate
attribute count from field identity or document length, and do not measure LLM
selection. The archived query-level runs, paired bootstrap analysis, input hashes,
and environment manifest are distributed with the Python package. Full-corpus
replication and randomized-order controls remain open.
